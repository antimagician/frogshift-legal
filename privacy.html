Privacy Policy

How Frog Shift handles local gameplay information, advertising consent, AdMob, and App Store purchases.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

PrivacyTermsSupport

1. Overview

Frog Shift is a mobile puzzle game. No Frog Shift account is required to play. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app, including information processed by third-party services used for advertising and purchases.

2. Information stored by Frog Shift

Frog Shift stores gameplay and preference information locally on your device, such as level progress, star ratings, settings, tutorial state, audio preferences, and limited ad-frequency state used to avoid showing ads too often.

Frog Shift does not require you to create an account and does not operate its own account database for this information.

3. Advertising

The free version of Frog Shift may display interstitial advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its partners may process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers where permitted, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices in order to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

The exact data processed can depend on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google's ad-serving mode.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and privacy choices

Frog Shift uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required. The app checks the current consent status before requesting ads.

Where required by the consent framework, Frog Shift provides a privacy-options entry in Settings so you can review or change applicable choices.

You may also control system-level privacy and advertising settings through iOS Settings.

5. In-App Purchases

Frog Shift offers an optional non-consumable “Remove Ads” purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Frog Shift does not receive your payment-card details.

The app checks verified StoreKit transaction entitlements to determine whether Remove Ads is owned.

For information about Apple's data practices, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Data sharing

Frog Shift does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate app features, including Google for advertising/consent services and Apple for App Store purchases and related platform services.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Retention and deletion

Local Frog Shift gameplay and settings data generally remains on your device until it is changed, reset, or the app is deleted, subject to normal iOS behavior.

Advertising and purchase-related information processed by Google or Apple is retained according to those providers' policies and applicable law.

Because Frog Shift does not maintain a user account database, there may be no developer-hosted personal profile to delete. For third-party data requests, you may need to use the privacy tools provided by Google or Apple.

8. International use

Frog Shift may be available in multiple countries. Privacy rights and consent requirements vary by jurisdiction. Where applicable, consent and privacy-choice mechanisms are presented through the services integrated in the app.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Frog Shift features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version.
